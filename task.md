# Diyargezen Project Context & Guidelines

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: TTRPG (Tabletop Role Playing Game) players, character creators, and Game Masters (GMs) playing Pathfinder 1st Edition (PF1e).
- **Usage Context**: Real-time character creation, level-up progression during or between sessions, offline-first mobile/desktop sheet inspection, and live PDF export.
- **Job to Be Done**: Effortlessly build legal, rule-compliant PF1e characters, automate complex stat recalculation (BAB, saves, AC, skills, modifiers), manage inventory/feats/traits, and export live fillable PDF sheets without losing state offline or online.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Wise, epic, authoritative yet flexible Game Master voice.
- **3-Word Personality**: Nostaljik (Nostalgic), Epik (Epic), Zengin (Detailed).
- **Emotional Goals**: Evoke wonder, confidence in rule accuracy, tactical clarity, and immersive dark fantasy atmosphere.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Atmospheric & Immersive Dark Fantasy. Deep obsidian (`#0f0f1a`) and slate (`#1a1a2e`) backgrounds, warm gold filigree (`#c9a84c`), bronze details (`#8b7355`), and arcane violet (`#7c6ef7`) spell highlights.
- **Typography**: Modern Outfit sans-serif paired with crisp dark fantasy contrast text (`#f0e6d2` primary cream, `#d4c5a9` secondary beige).
- **References**: High-end TTRPG codexes, polished Foundry VTT dark themes, and immersive fantasy character sheets.
- **Anti-References**: Generic light-mode corporate SaaS dashboards, flat neon bootstrap layouts, or sterile monochrome spreadsheets.

### Design Principles
1. **Epic Dark Fantasy Aesthetics**: Every screen should feel like consulting an ancient arcane grimoire or a warm tavern ledger, utilizing rich dark backgrounds, subtle gold borders, and glowing magic highlights.
2. **Tactical Clarity & Zero Clutter**: Despite the high density of Pathfinder 1e rules, information must be structured logically with clear visual hierarchy, distinct card containers, and intuitive tab/scroll organization.
3. **Soft-Block GM Empowerment**: Never hard-lock the player out of creative choices; provide intelligent rule validation warnings with explicit GM override flags and manual stat modifier options.
4. **Offline-First & Responsive Resilience**: Ensure smooth, micro-animated interactions that function seamlessly whether running on web browser or desktop client with local SQLite sync.
