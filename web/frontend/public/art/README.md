# Diyargezen görseli

`diyargezen-realm.webp`: bu proje için OpenAI görüntü üretimi aracıyla oluşturulan
özgün fantastik diyar illüstrasyonu. 1916 × 821, 187402 byte. PNG'den WebP'ye yalnızca
format/sıkıştırma dönüşümü uygulandı; harici site görseli indirilmedi.

Kullanım: giriş ekranı ve karakter mahzeni hero arka planı. Mevcut logo korunur.
Görsel dekoratiftir; okunabilirlik koyu katman ve ayrı HTML metinlerle sağlanır.

Üretim istemi:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic wide website hero background
> for Diyargezen, a modern Pathfinder 1st Edition character management app.
> An original lush high-fantasy realm at blue-hour dawn, an ancient stone archway
> opening toward a luminous mountain citadel and winding river, tiny distant warm
> lights, emerald vegetation and atmospheric teal mist with restrained warm gold
> highlights. Painterly premium fantasy concept art, elegant and inviting, rich
> depth, no excessive detail noise. Wide landscape composition with a large
> quieter dark atmospheric area on the left for readable website heading,
> architectural focal point toward the right. Deep ink navy, emerald and antique
> gold. No lettering, logos, UI, watermark or recognizable franchise characters.
> Production web background, not a website screenshot.
